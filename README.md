### 🔬 About Me
* 🌱 I'm an M.S. Fellow in Microbiology at **Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU)**, Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a **B.Sc. in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry** (CGPA 3.54 / 4.00).
* 🦠 My research interests lie at the intersection of **veterinary microbiology**, **antimicrobial resistance (AMR)** and **One Health**, focusing on bacterial pathogens, host–microbe interactions, gut health and poultry health.
* 🧪 Research Assistant at the **Association of Coding, Technology and Genomics (ACTG)** (2025 – present) and formerly in the **Histology / Histopathology Laboratory, SAU** (2023 – 2025).
* 🩺 Veterinary Medical Officer (contract), **Dhaka North City Corporation** (May 2026) · International Veterinary Medicine Internship, **Universiti Putra Malaysia** (Jan 2026).
* 💻 Currently developing skills in **bacterial genomics**, **whole-genome sequencing** and **reproducible bioinformatics workflows** for AMR surveillance in animal and food systems.
